# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in VoltPanel a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and technically honest.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Focus discussion on the project and user impact.
- Respect privacy and responsible disclosure.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful outcomes directly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing another person's private information
- Publicly disclosing unpatched security vulnerabilities
- Deliberately disrupting project infrastructure or community spaces

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and contributions that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.
